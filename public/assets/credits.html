Art and asset credits

Let's Scream Together

Current Halloween world

The 261 models in the three Let’s Scream Together asset packs were supplied by the project owner. It provides the current characters, monsters, structures, props, traps, items and pixel textures. First pack manifest and second pack manifest, and forest pack manifest.

The supplied REWORK_GUIDE defines the interface and pixel-mask logo. Big Shoulders Display, Archivo and Silkscreen are self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License.

The Hollow

Creature model, source textures and animations: Aaron Neal, “Demon”. Used under the author's free-use permission with credit. Original release and permission.

Adapted for this game with browser compression, retimed animations, adjusted materials and a first-person claw pose. The source creature was created by Aaron Neal.

Survivors
